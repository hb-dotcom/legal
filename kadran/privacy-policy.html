Kadran · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 18, 2026  ·  Last updated: April 18, 2026

Kadran ("we", "our", or "us") is an iOS application developed by Ark Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Kadran.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique Apple user identifier and, optionally, your name and email address (depending on your Apple privacy settings).
  • Watch photos: Photos you capture within the app are uploaded to our secure cloud storage (Supabase) for AI processing. These images are stored under your account and are not shared with third parties beyond the AI processing pipeline.
  • Purchase data: In-app purchase transactions are processed by Apple and RevenueCat. We store only the necessary identifiers to credit your account (transaction IDs, product IDs, credit amounts).
  • Usage data: We may collect anonymized usage data (e.g., feature usage frequency) to improve the app. No personally identifiable information is included.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your account.
  • To process your watch photos using AI and deliver results.
  • To manage your credit balance and verify in-app purchases.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany). All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted through row-level security policies — only you can access your own photos and account data.

AI processing is performed via fal.ai. Photos are transmitted to fal.ai solely for the purpose of generating enhanced images and are not retained by fal.ai beyond the processing window.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Apple Sign In — authentication
  • Supabase — database and file storage
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management
  • fal.ai — AI image processing

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data to any third party.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account through the app, all associated data — including your photos, credit history, and account information — is permanently and irrevocably deleted within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data (available directly in Settings → Delete Account).
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

7. Children's Privacy

Kadran is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

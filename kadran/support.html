Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits work?

Each credit allows the app to process one photo with AI. You start with 1 free credit after completing onboarding. Additional credits can be purchased in-app: Starter (6 credits), Popular (30 credits), or Best Value (60 credits).

A photo failed to process. Did I lose my credit?

No. If processing fails due to a system error, your credit is automatically refunded to your account. If your balance doesn't update within a few minutes, please contact us.

I made a purchase but my credits didn't appear.

This can occasionally happen due to network delays. Go to Settings and tap "Restore Purchases" — this will sync your purchase history and update your balance. If the issue persists, email us with your Apple order number.

The AI results don't look right. What can I do?

Result quality depends heavily on photo conditions. For best results: shoot in natural or diffused light, keep the watch on a neutral background, and follow the angle guides shown in the app. If a result is unsatisfactory, you can re-process the same photo for that angle from the result screen.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings inside the app and tap "Delete Account". This permanently removes all your data including photos, credits, and purchase history. This action cannot be undone.

Can I use Kadran for watches other than my own?

Yes — Kadran is designed for any second-hand watch that you are authorized to photograph and sell. There are no restrictions on the watch brand or model.

Is my data shared with anyone?

Your photos are processed by our AI provider (fal.ai) and then stored securely under your account. We do not sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

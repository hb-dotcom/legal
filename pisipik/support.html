FAQ

My credits were charged but no portrait was generated.

Credits are automatically refunded if a generation fails. If you don't see the refund within a few minutes, email us and we'll fix it manually.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to submit a request.

The portrait quality doesn't look right.

For best results, use a clear, well-lit photo of your cat's face. Avoid blurry or dark photos. You can retry the generation with a different photo.

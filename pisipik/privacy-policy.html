Pisipik Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 9, 2026

This policy explains what data Pisipik collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Data Controller

Pisipik (independent developer product) is responsible for the data processing described in this policy.

Contact: contact.arklabs@gmail.com

1) Data We Collect

  • Account data: When you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple-provided user identifier and, if you choose to share it, your email address. If you use the app anonymously, only a technical anonymous ID is created.
  • Pet photos: Photos you upload for AI portrait generation. These are sent to our server for processing and are not stored permanently after generation is complete.
  • Generated portraits: AI-generated images are stored in your collection so you can access them later.
  • Purchase data: Credit pack purchases (type, amount, date). Payment details (card number, etc.) are never stored by Pisipik; they are handled exclusively by Apple.
  • Credit balance: Your current credit count, managed server-side.
  • Device diagnostics: Anonymous crash logs (error message, module, device platform, app version). These logs do not contain personal information.

2) How We Use Your Data

  • Generate AI pet portraits from the photos you upload.
  • Maintain your portrait collection so you can view and share your images.
  • Track and manage your credit balance for in-app purchases.
  • Verify purchase status and provide purchased credits.
  • Detect and fix app errors (anonymous diagnostics).

3) Data Sharing

Your data is only processed by services necessary for the app to function:

Service Purpose Data Type
Supabase Authentication and cloud storage Account ID, credit balance, generated portrait references
Sign in with Apple Apple ID authentication (iOS) Account credentials required for sign-in
fal.ai AI image generation Pet photos (for processing only; not stored by fal.ai after generation)
RevenueCat In-app purchase management User ID, purchase status, credit pack info

We do not sell or share data with advertising networks.

4) Photo Processing

When you upload a pet photo, it is sent to our server, which forwards it to the AI generation service (fal.ai). The uploaded photo is used solely for generating your portrait and is not retained after the generation process is complete.

Generated portraits are stored in your collection until you delete them or your account.

5) Data Retention

  • Uploaded pet photos are processed in real-time and not permanently stored on our servers.
  • Generated portraits and account data are retained until you request account deletion.
  • Diagnostic logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days and are automatically deleted.

6) Age Requirement

Pisipik is designed for users aged 4 and older. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 4.

7) Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at contact.arklabs@gmail.com.

Upon deletion, all your generated portraits, credit balance, and account information will be permanently removed.

8) Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as the product evolves. The current version will always be available on this page.
